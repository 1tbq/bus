How this works

You are testing a product. Every answer either extends its life or wears it down.

  • Correct answer: +6% lifespan and 100 points.
  • Wrong answer: the product loses 16% lifespan.
  • Streaks: 3 in a row doubles your points. 5 in a row triples them.
  • Product failure: if lifespan reaches zero the product dies. You restart the current round with a fresh 60%.

Four rounds

  • Round 1 — Planned or not? Tell a design decision apart from ordinary wear.
  • Round 2 — Name the type. Built-in, perceived or software.
  • Round 3 — Myth or fact.
  • Round 4 — Gain or cost. Who ends up better off, and who pays.
