Your shift

Every customer at the counter has a claim. Your job is to work out what the Australian Consumer Law actually says, not what the shop policy says.

  • Correct call: the claim value is recovered for the customer and added to your ledger.
  • Wrong call: the customer walks away with nothing and a complaint is logged against you.
  • Streaks: 3 correct in a row doubles the value you recover. 5 in a row triples it.
  • Your rating at the end depends on how few complaints you logged.

Four rounds

  • Round 1 — Covered or not? Does the law protect this customer at all?
  • Round 2 — Major or minor? This decides who chooses the remedy.
  • Round 3 — Myth or fact.
  • Round 4 — Guarantee or warranty? Where does this right come from?
