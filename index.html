Case File 3: Remedy Sorter

0 / 8

Decide what kind of problem each customer has. This tells you what remedy they can get.

Major failure: the customer chooses a refund or replacement. Minor failure: the seller can choose to repair, replace or refund. Change of mind: no legal right to a refund. It is up to store policy.
