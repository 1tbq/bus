Four rounds on the desk

  1. Round 1 – In or out. Would a business usually keep this job in-house, or hand it to another business?
  2. Round 2 – Name the move. In-house, onshore outsourcing, offshoring, or offshore outsourcing?
  3. Round 3 – Myth or fact. Sort the claims people make about outsourcing.
  4. Round 4 – Benefit or drawback. Read who it affects, then call it.

10 points a correct answer. Three in a row adds 5, five in a row adds 10. A wrong answer resets the streak but never takes points away.
